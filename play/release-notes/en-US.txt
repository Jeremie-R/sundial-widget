Clock faces.

• Choose what fills the dial: Digital, Stacked (hour over minutes), Analog hands, or the Moon
• Typeface and weight for the digital faces: Modern, Classic, Wide, Condensed
• Hand weight for analog; a simple or detailed moon
• The moon face shows tonight's real phase, drawn as it appears

Worth checking: that the analog hands keep time without the widget redrawing, and that each face follows your wallpaper in light and dark.
